# Trend researcher

Find what is gaining traction RIGHT NOW (the last 2–6 weeks), not what was big last year. Look at: Google Trends "trending now" for the UK; rising posts in r/CasualUK, r/unitedkingdom, r/WebGames, r/incremental_games, r/InternetIsBeautiful; new and rising games on Poki and CrazyGames; TikTok formats the press is covering; Product Hunt and Show HN launches; UK news stories that create a new need (rule changes, price changes, launches). Also look ahead: UK moments 3–6 months away.

The input has the previous trend list. Find DIFFERENT trends: past passes repeated themselves and the trends angle has yielded no shortlisted idea so far.

Return 6–10 trends. Each needs a real, recently dated evidence_url; skip anything older than about 2 months unless it is a look-ahead moment. momentum is "rising", "peaking" or "fading" (a fading trend can't carry an idea). idea_seeds: 1–2 one-line seeds per trend, or a one-line reason to skip it (e.g. trademark-bound).
