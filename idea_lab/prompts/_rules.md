## Rules for every Idea Lab agent (Michael's, they outrank everything else)

- Context: Michael builds small websites on his own, mostly in about a week as a static page (game, tool or reference), plus some bigger builds of 2–6 weeks. They are hosted free or cheaply (Cloudflare Workers/Pages, D1/R2/KV, Supabase free tier), grow with free traffic (search, sharing, portals, Reddit) and earn from display ads later.
- REAL URLS ONLY. Never invent a source, competitor, price, domain availability or number. If you can't verify something, write "unverified". A guess must be labelled as a guess or an estimate.
- NO FAMOUS-GAME RESKINS. A famous free daily or casual game (Wordle, NYT Connections / Only Connect's wall, Strands, Spelling Bee, Worldle, Heardle/Bandle, Tradle, MapTap, enclose.horse, Queens, Zip, 2048, Sudoku…) with a UK theme, new content or a new name on top is not a new idea. Popularity of the original is not demand for the copy: label it "proxy: demand for the original", never evidence.
- NAME AND DOMAIN FIRST: names that are already an existing game or product are a problem.
- Sites are judged on return visits at weeks 4 and 8, not early ad money. No paid advertising.
- Ideas must be legal, safe for a general audience, and must not use other people's trademarks or IP in names.
- Michael's decisions and his own words (in the taste summary) outrank everything else you are told.
- When searching for rivals, always also check Google Play / the App Store, Product Hunt and plain web tools, not only "<thing>le" games: past runs missed rivals there.
- Licence traps already found (don't propose these as free data): YouGov data is non-commercial; Open Plaques' data dumps date from 2021; Anefo photos are CC BY-SA, not CC0; FixMyStreet states no reuse licence; NLS historic map tiles come via MapTiler, whose free tier is non-commercial; Spotify's terms ban quiz games; the Official Charts Company licenses Number One data commercially; BBC Desert Island Discs data and trademark can't be used; Football DataCo licenses fixtures. Cloudflare Pages caps free projects at 20,000 files and 25 MiB per file (big media goes in R2).
- Keep text fields short and concrete. British English.
